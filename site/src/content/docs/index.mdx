## What you can do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Keep notes as plain files" icon="document">
    A vault is any folder. Each note is a `.md` or `.mdx` file, so git, sync tools and other editors keep
    working with your notes. [Get started](/BoostNote/get-started/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Write with live preview" icon="pencil">
    Headings, tables, task lists, math, Mermaid diagrams and images show as you type. Type `/` to
    insert a block. [Write notes](/BoostNote/guides/write-notes/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organise and find" icon="magnifier">
    Use folders with colours, tags, stars and pins. Search the text of your notes.
    [Organise notes](/BoostNote/guides/organise-notes/), [Find notes](/BoostNote/guides/find-notes/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Capture a thought fast" icon="rocket">
    Open a scratch note with one shortcut. Turn on the Quick capture hotkey in Preferences to open one
    from any app. Save it to a vault later.
    [Quick capture](/BoostNote/guides/quick-capture/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share and export" icon="external">
    Export a note as HTML, PDF or Markdown, or export a folder or the whole vault as a website.
    [Share and export](/BoostNote/guides/share-and-export/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use it from the terminal" icon="seti:shell">
    The `boostnote` command and its MCP server read and change your notes from scripts and AI
    tools. [Terminal and AI tools](/BoostNote/guides/terminal-and-ai/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Boostnote runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. The screenshots in this guide come from macOS. On
Windows and Linux, the app uses your system font.
