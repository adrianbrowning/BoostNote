This page shows how to find a note in your vault and how to find text in the open note.

## Search all notes

Search finds the notes that contain all the words that you type.

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, click **All notes**.
2. Click the **Search notes** box above the note list.
3. Type one or more words.
4. Click a note in the results to open it.

</Steps>

<Shot
  name="find-notes-search"
  alt="The note list with the search results for the word design, and the Website redesign note open"
/>

The best results are at the top. A word in the title or in a tag counts more than a word in the text.
Pinned notes stay at the top of the results.

To find only notes with a tag, type `#` before the word, for example `#recipe`.

To clear the search, delete the text in the search box.

<Aside type="tip">
  Search looks only in the list that you selected in the sidebar. To search the full vault, click **All
  notes** first.
</Aside>

## Browse your notes

The sidebar selects which notes the note list shows.

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, click one of these items:
   - **All notes** shows every note in the vault.
   - **Starred** shows the notes that you starred.
   - A folder shows the notes in that folder.
   - A tag shows the notes with that tag.
2. Click a note in the list to open it.

</Steps>

<Shot
  name="find-notes-tag"
  alt="The recipe tag selected in the sidebar, the three recipe notes in the list, and Pasta al limone open"
/>

The number next to each item is the number of its notes. A folder shows only the notes directly in
it, not the notes in its subfolders. Click **▸** next to a folder to show its subfolders.

To star, pin or tag a note, see [Organise notes](/BoostNote/guides/organise-notes/).

## Sort the note list

<Steps>

1. Click the **Sort notes** menu next to the search box.
2. Choose **Updated**, **Created** or **Title**.

</Steps>

**Updated** and **Created** show the newest notes first. **Title** sorts the notes from A to Z.
Pinned notes always stay at the top.

You cannot sort search results. They are always in order of best match.

## Read the note previews

Each row in the note list shows:

- The title of the note.
- **★** if the note is starred, and **📌** if it is pinned.
- The first part of the text of the note.
- The date the note was last updated. When you sort by **Created**, the row shows the date the note was
  created.
- The tags of the note.

## Find text in the open note

<Steps>

1. Open a note.
2. Choose **Edit › Find…** (<Shortcut command="find" />). The Find bar opens below the note.
3. Type the text that you want to find. Boostnote highlights each match.
4. Press <Shortcut keys="Enter" /> or click **next** to go to the next match. Press
   <Shortcut keys="Shift+Enter" /> or click **previous** to go back.
5. Press <Shortcut keys="Escape" /> or click **×** to close the Find bar.

</Steps>

<Shot
  name="find-notes-find-bar"
  alt="The Find bar below the Markdown tour note, with the word note highlighted in the text"
/>

The Find bar has more options:

- **all** selects all the matches.
- **match case** finds only text with the same capital and small letters.
- **regexp** lets you type a regular expression.
- **by word** finds only whole words.
- Type new text in the **Replace** box. Then click **replace** to change one match, or **replace all**
  to change all the matches.
